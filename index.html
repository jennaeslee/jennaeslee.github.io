01 / About

About Me

I'm an Atmospheric Science master's student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with an interdisciplinary background in atmospheric science, climate data analysis, and geospatial analysis.

Through academic projects, I've explored climate model data, air pollution trends, and wildfire risk assessment using GIS. I'm particularly interested in numerical weather prediction, machine learning, and computational approaches to analyzing complex atmospheric systems.

My long-term goal is to connect weather science with quantitative decision-making, particularly in weather risk assessment, energy demand forecasting, and financial applications of weather data.

ACADEMIC PROJECT

Wildfire Risk & Evacuation Prioritization

California · ArcGIS Pro · Spatial Analysis

Developed a composite risk index to identify high-priority wildfire management and evacuation areas in California by integrating wildfire hazard, population, and road network information.

Examined geographic patterns of wildfire risk and potential evacuation bottlenecks, including areas in the Sierra Nevada foothills.

GIS ArcGIS Pro Risk Assessment

ACADEMIC PROJECT

SO₂ Pollution Trends & Analysis

Climate Modeling · GFDL-ESM4

Analyzed historical and future sulfur dioxide (SO₂) pollution patterns across the contiguous United States using climate model data and population statistics.

Investigated long-term pollution trends and the relationship between population patterns and per-capita emissions.

Climate Data Statistical Analysis Regression

IN PROGRESS

Heating & Cooling Degree Days

Climate Data · Energy Demand · Python

Analyzing long-term heating and cooling degree days across U.S. cities to investigate climate trends, year-to-year variability, and temperature-related energy demand.
